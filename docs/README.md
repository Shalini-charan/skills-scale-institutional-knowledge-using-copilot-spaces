# OctoAcme Project Management Documentation

Welcome to the OctoAcme Project Management guide—a comprehensive resource for running projects with clarity, accountability, and iterative delivery.

## Quick Overview

OctoAcme follows a structured yet flexible project lifecycle based on these core principles:
- **Customer-first**: Prioritize customer value and usability
- **Iterative delivery**: Deliver small, testable increments
- **Clear ownership**: Each project has a named Project Manager (PM) and Product Lead
- **Data-informed decisions**: Measure impact and iterate based on evidence
- **Psychological safety**: Encourage feedback and learning

## OctoAcme Project Management Approach

OctoAcme follows a structured, five-phase project lifecycle designed to deliver customer value through iterative, data-informed execution. The process begins with **Initiation**, where new ideas are validated through a lightweight Project One-pager that confirms business need, identifies stakeholders, and establishes success metrics. Once approved by the Product Lead and sponsors, projects move into **Planning**, where work is broken down into shippable increments with clear acceptance criteria, dependencies are mapped, and a release timeline is established.

The core delivery phase, **Execution & Tracking**, keeps projects on track through daily standups, weekly syncs, and sprint-based workflows. Following successful delivery, projects proceed to **Release & Deployment** with standardized pre-release checklists, smoke testing, and documented rollback procedures. Finally, teams conduct **Retrospectives** to capture learnings and drive continuous improvement, creating a feedback loop that strengthens future project execution.

The organizational structure centers on three core roles working in close collaboration: **Project Managers** coordinate delivery activities and manage schedules, risks, and communications; **Product Managers** define what should be built and own the vision and backlog prioritization; and **Developers** implement features, write tests, and identify technical risks. This clear ownership model is reinforced by a communication cadence that includes weekly PM-to-PdM syncs, twice-weekly team standups, monthly stakeholder updates, and ad-hoc escalations as needed. Quality and risk management are embedded throughout, with every project maintaining a risk register, conducting security scanning in CI, and following documented escalation paths to resolve blockers efficiently.

## Core Roles

- **Project Manager (PM)**: Coordinates delivery, schedules, risks, communications
- **Product Manager (PdM)**: Defines outcomes, prioritizes backlog, measures success
- **Developers**: Implement features, collaborate on design and testing
- **QA/Testing**: Validate quality and acceptance criteria
- **Stakeholders**: Provide inputs and approvals

## Communication Cadence

- **Weekly sync** between PM + PdM
- **Twice-weekly standups** for delivery team
- **Monthly stakeholder updates**
- **Ad-hoc escalations** as needed
